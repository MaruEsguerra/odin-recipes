Japanese Beef Curry

Description

Japanese Curry Rice is an extremely popular dish for all ages in Japan. It is considered one of the country's national dishes along with ramen and gyoza. This recipe is one of the variations of Japanese curry.

This recipe is inspired from Nami's justonecookbook.

Ingredients

  • 283g onion
  • 1 tablespoon of unsalted butter
  • 1 tablespoon of neutral oil
  • 155g potato
  • 115g carrot
  • 40g cremini mushrooms
  • 1/4 teaspoon of grated ginger, with juice
  • Half of a garlic clove, minced
  • 285g beef
  • Salt, to taste
  • Ground black pepper, to taste
  • 1/2 tablespoon of all purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup of red wine
  • 1 beef broth cube, dissolved in 2 cups of water
  • 1/4 tablespoon of filipino curry powder
  • 1/2 tablespoon of tomato paste
  • 1/2 tablespoon of honey
  • 1/2 tablespoon worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tablespoon milk
  • Half a bay leaf
  • 1/2 of a package of Japanese curry roux

Steps

  1. Prepare onions: Thinly slice the onion. Preheat a large heavy pot over medium heat, then add some unsalted butter and half the neutral oil. Add the onions, stir to coat, and sauté for 20-25 minutes, stirring occasionally. Once wilted, reduce heat to medium-low to avoid burning. Cook until tender, translucent, and slightly caramelized. Set aside.
  2. Prep vegetables:
    • Peel and cut potatoes and carrots into bite-sized pieces. Soak potatoes in water for 15 minutes, then drain.
    • Clean and slice mushrooms thinly. (Tip: Mushrooms absorb moisture, so avoid washing—brush them off, or give a quick rinse if necessary.)
    • Grate ginger and mince garlic.
  3. Prepare beef: Cut beef into cubes and season with salt and pepper. Lightly coat in flour.
  4. Sear beef: Heat a pan over medium-high heat, add remaining oil and butter. Sear beef in batches to avoid overcrowding, cooking each side until browned and crusty. Transfer seared meat to a plate.
  5. Deglaze pan: Add red wine to the pan and scrape up the browned bits with a wooden spatula. Set aside the deglazed liquid.
  6. Cook curry:
    • Reheat the onion pot over medium heat, add garlic and ginger. Stir in curry powder and tomato paste, cooking for 1 minute.
    • Add seared beef, plate juices, and deglazed pan liquid. Stir and cook for 3 minutes to evaporate the alcohol.
    • Add carrots, mushrooms, and enough beef broth to cover. Bring to a boil with the lid on.
  7. Simmer: Skim off any scum and fat. Add honey, Worcestershire sauce, milk, and bay leaf. Cover and simmer on low heat for 1 hour and 45 minutes, adding more broth if needed to cover ingredients.
  8. Finish cooking: Add potatoes and simmer for 15 minutes, or until tender. Discard bay leaf.
  9. Add curry roux: Stir in Japanese curry roux. Simmer on low for 3-5 minutes, stirring often until thickened. If too thick, add water. If too soupy, simmer uncovered until the desired consistency.
  10. Serve: Plate the curry with rice and enjoy!
