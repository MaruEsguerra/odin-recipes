Nama Chocolate

Description

Nama Chocolate is rich, smooth, and moist, with a silky texture that practically melts in your mouth. The good news is that you don't have to fly to Japan to enjory the luxurious sweet. With this recipe, you can make your chocolate dream come true today!

This recipe is taken from Nami's justonecookbook.

Ingredients

  • 400g good-quality dark chocolate, about 70% cacao
  • 200ml heavy whipping cream
  • 1 tablespoon of brandy
  • Dutch-process cocoa powder, to coat

Steps

  1. Chop the chocolate: With a sharp knife, chop the chocolate into smaller pieces to help them melt faster and more evenly.
  2. Prepare the baking dish: Line an 8x8-inch baking dish with parchment paper. Using the right-sized tray is important; if the tray is too big, the chocolate will be too flat.
  3. Heat the cream:
    • Add the heavy whipping cream to a small saucepan over medium heat. Heat it until it's almost boiling.
    • Lightly whisk the cream to evenly distribute the heat. Avoid letting it boil, as overheating could cause the chocolate to separate.
  4. Remove the cream from the heat: Once you see small bubbles forming around the edges of the saucepan, remove it from the heat.
  5. Melt the chocolate:
    • Add the chopped chocolate to the hot cream. Stir with a dry silicone spatula to combine. Avoid any contact with water or steam, as it may cause the chocolate to seize.
    • Stir until the chocolate is fully melted and the mixture is smooth without lumps.
  6. Add brandy: Stir the brandy into the chocolate mixture until fully incorporated.
  7. Pour into the dish: Pour the smooth chocolate mixture into the prepared baking dish. Use a silicone spatula to smooth out the surface.
  8. Tap the dish:
    • Gently tap the dish on the counter to ensure the chocolate is evenly distributed.
    • Refrigerate the dish for 4-5 hours, or until firm.
  9. Remove from the dish:
    • Once chilled, lift the chocolate from the baking dish using the parchment paper.
    • Peel away the parchment paper from the sides.
  10. Trim the edges: Use a sharp knife to trim any excess chocolate from the edges, ensuring clean, sharp corners.
  11. Cut into strips: Warm a knife with a hot towel, then cut the chocolate slab into 1-inch strips. Clean and warm the knife between cuts for sharp, clean lines.
  12. Cut into cubes: Cut the chocolate strips into cubes, cleaning and warming the knife before each cut.
  13. Dust with cocoa powder: Use a fine-mesh sieve or sugar duster to sprinkle the cubes with Dutch-process cocoa powder.
  14. Serve: Place on a dish and let everyone help themselves!
